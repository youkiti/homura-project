# We buy records of completed systematic reviews

To build a dataset for evaluating systematic review support tools, the HOMURA project buys records of past systematic reviews (protocol, search strategies, screening decisions, and data extraction and risk of bias assessments).

**The application form and the contract are in Japanese only.** To apply, you need to read and agree to the Japanese contract. The Japanese page is the authoritative version.

## Summary

- Payment: JPY 15,000 per review, for reviews with all five files listed below
- Cap: 100 reviews in total
- Selection: applications are accepted in the order received until the cap is reached. An application for several reviews counts each review in that order. The call closes when the cap is reached
- Members of the project team are treated the same as other applicants and receive no priority
- Contracting party: Tokai National Higher Education and Research System (Nagoya University). No seal or signature is needed

## Eligible reviews

1. The review is published (has a DOI or PMID, including reviews published as part of a clinical practice guideline)
2. The review was completed before 7 September 2026
3. All five files listed below are available. Reviews without risk of bias assessment or data extraction (such as scoping reviews) are not eligible
4. A registered (e.g. PROSPERO) or published protocol exists
5. Neither your current institution nor your institution at the time of the review holds rights or control over the data
6. Your co-authors and co-reviewers agree to the provision

Each review is bought only once. If several co-authors apply for the same review, only the first application is accepted.

## Files required for each review

1. Protocol
2. Search strategies for all databases, with search dates and hit counts (all rounds if the search was updated)
3. Title and abstract screening decisions: each reviewer's individual decisions and the consensus decisions. Consensus decisions alone are accepted only when the individual decisions no longer exist
4. Full-text screening decisions (same as above). If the final decisions are not kept in the screening tool, the study lists in the published review can be used instead
5. Data extraction and risk of bias assessments (same as above). Do not include text copied from the published review

Files should be CSV or Excel. Remove abstracts, controlled vocabulary (MeSH, Emtree), and reviewer names (replace them with codes such as A and B). We do not accept RIS files, abstracts, or article PDFs.

## Steps

1. Submit the application form (do not send files yet)
2. We email a purchase order. The contract is formed at this point
3. Upload the files within four weeks of the purchase order
4. We check the files within 10 days
5. Sign the delivery note and invoice we prepare. Payment is made within 60 days of receiving the invoice

## Use and release of the data

- We plan to release the screening, extraction and risk of bias data and the protocols under CC BY 4.0
- Bibliographic data we release are retrieved independently from free public sources. Abstracts are not released
- The data may be processed by generative AI services, including services provided outside Japan, and used to train or improve models
