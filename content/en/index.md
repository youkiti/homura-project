# HOMURA project

Human-Overseen Models for Unified Review Automation

Every stage of a systematic review (search design, screening, data extraction) needs specialist training and many hours of work. The HOMURA project uses large language models to help people without that training start a sound systematic review.

We do not hand the review over to AI. We make visible where AI was used and what humans checked, and we aim to reduce the total human workload within that frame.

## Tools

All tools are open source.

| Stage | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Search design | [SR Query Builder](https://github.com/youkiti/sr-query-builder-plugin) |
| Title and abstract screening | [TiAb Review](https://github.com/youkiti/tiab-review-plugin) |
| Data extraction | [SR Data Extraction](https://youkiti.github.io/sr-data-extraction-plugin/) |

## How we work

1. We build a benchmark from records of past systematic reviews and measure how the tools perform. The [call for systematic review data](sr-data/) supports this step.
2. We test the tools in a randomized controlled trial with people who conduct systematic reviews.
